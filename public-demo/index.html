THE MECHANICS, IN PLAIN SIGHT

            
Small rules.
Meaningful tradeoffs.

            

              Three crops differ in cost, growth time and yield. Rain wets the
              soil and fills the reservoir. A dry crop misses a growth day; six
              dry growth days cause it to wither.
            

          

          
    
            
  1. 
              Grow before the clock runs out.
              
    
                Radishes need 3 growth days, beans 5 and squash 7. A harvest
                consumes another day. Mature plants lose yield after waiting
                more than three days.
              
    
            
    2. 
            
  2. 
              Balance cash, water and storage.
              
    
                Watering uses 2 reservoir units. Four water cost 3 coins, capped
                by capacity. Stored crops can be sold at the currently visible
                price. Farm work earns 1 coin.
              
    
            
    3. 
            
  3. 
              Separate an outcome from a claim.
              
    
                Final assets are coins plus stored crop value at closing prices.
                Unharvested plants have zero closing value. This teaching
                environment is not the official Kaggriculture simulator.
