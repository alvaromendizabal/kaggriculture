## Exclusions with a domain reason

Study 2's [measured findings](relationship_findings.md) separate positive component effects,
harmful conditional effects, non-activation and absent trajectory coverage. None closes a
family automatically. [Study 3](market_findings.md) adds tested market/delivery interventions
and an independently audited 933-column bank. All descriptors remain provisional; the
harmful holding rule is not adopted, and delivery's coin gain is not broad win-rate proof.
The [history follow-up](market_history_research.md) adds 333 independently screened
candidates (178 varying, 155 constant, 12 nonconstant duplicate groups). They remain
provisional. Its conservation audit is identification evidence, not a new policy ablation,
and it does not claim the prior and new banks were jointly screened.

The [fourth study](supply_research.md) now jointly screens all 1,308 candidates:
754 varying, 554 constant, 74 duplicate groups and 1,866 high-correlation pairs.
Stored-supply history scored 0.625 pooled versus bank's 0.375; all extra wins were
narrow mirror-match wins, with no score gain against the other frozen opponent.
Cash tightened identification without changing any paired history/cash action
sequence. Neither broad competitive superiority nor final feature selection is
established.

The [fifth study](livestock_research.md) adds 234 resource descriptors, 192 varying.
Its 1,500 supported candidates are jointly screened: 1,205 varying, 295 constant,
157 duplicate groups and 1,775 high-correlation pairs. Seventy of 78 earlier animal
descriptors now vary. The project union is 1,542; 42 cash descriptors are excluded
from this study because their contract does not cover own wheat purchases.
Selective feeding alone hurts score; coordinated care restores it. Fertilizer
improves margin over care but not wins. All 64 games pass independent action,
feature, transition and product-conservation checks. This is component evidence,
not final feature selection. Next priority is terminal-aware feed/service routing
and stronger unrelated opponents, followed by herd/crop mix and capital stress.

- Real soil, rainfall, weather, satellite imagery and calendar growing seasons: no supporting
  variables or transitions in the official game. External agronomic associations do not map
  to this simulator without an implemented causal mechanism.
- Seed reconstruction, future shop lists, evaluator rewards, hidden simulator objects and
  opponent private stocks: unavailable at decision time, so prohibited feature inputs.
- All-pairs polynomial expansion: no mechanistic justification and high duplication risk.
  Interactions are generated from explicit shared constraints or transition dependencies.

## Closure criteria

Every plausible high-value family needs a correct availability contract, coverage tests,
an actionable hypothesis and controlled component tests (or a documented domain exclusion).
Final candidates additionally need grouped development screening, robust primary-metric
benefit, seed/opponent sensitivity, runtime/memory checks, interaction audits and lineage.
Only then may the completion gate permit final optimization and untouched evaluation.
