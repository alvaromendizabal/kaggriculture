## Interpretation

The sprint changed the reproduction standard. A public notebook score, local panel win rate,
or exact source reconstruction is not sufficient evidence of transfer. A mechanism is now
considered competitively reproduced only when:

1. source/runtime parity is established where applicable;
2. the keeper reproduces recorded recent ladder outcomes exactly;
3. the candidate improves a chronological real-ladder holdout;
4. a responsive-opponent safety gate does not contradict the replay result.

Inspecting or benchmarking an external agent is not presented as reproduction of its hidden
optimizer or private training process. Third-party source remains attributed and outside this
public repository.
