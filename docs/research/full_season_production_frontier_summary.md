# Full-season production and worker frontier — compact public summary

The full executed research notebook is retained in the canonical AWS workspace because its embedded outputs make it output-heavy. This compact public record preserves the decision without bloating Git history.

## Question

Could the live-rating plateau be explained by a stable, repeated micro-defect such as simple worker reassignment, recurring wage waste, overflow, realized price, or missed harvesting?

## Result

The analysis completed **42 full-season public replay analyses** and returned **COMPLETE_NO_STABLE_DOMINANT_PATTERN**.

Movement consumed a large share of unit actions, but the optimistic task-graph reassignment ceiling was small relative to the official-rating gap. Loss mechanisms varied across time and between the two live submissions: production quantity, realized price/product mix, overflow, and harvest timing each appeared, but no single micro-fix dominated consistently.

## Decision

Do not spend another research round on simple worker reassignment or another isolated final-day/market threshold. The evidence supports a structurally larger hypothesis: full-season task-graph compatibility and representative current-meta validation are the missing capabilities.

The next experiment therefore refreshes only the newest replay delta and synthesizes/evaluates whole policy families from recent top-agent traces.
