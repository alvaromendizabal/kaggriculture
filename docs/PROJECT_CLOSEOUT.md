## Historical conclusions

The exact 48-episode reconstruction yielded **18 wins, 10 ties and 20 losses**, or **47.9% win/tie score**. That was substantially weaker than the fixed local panel suggested. I replaced that selection reference and retained rejected transfers and intervention studies in the record.

The strongest historical returned project rating was **2048.7**. The September 30, 2026 latest-two pair was **1691.0 / 1516.2**. These official ratings are separate from local win/tie scores and the synthetic demo's economic outcomes.

The native harvest/service study ended during post-game diagnostics. Its scientific result is unresolved. A prepared recovery artifact is not counted as a successful competitive rerun. Recorded opponent tapes also cannot react to a changed agent, so replay fidelity alone does not establish responsive-opponent safety.

## Demonstration and publication boundary

The public lab computes decisions and outcomes over 24-day seasons and four plots. Three generic policies can be compared under identical seeded weather and price sequences. This makes legal observation, action availability and paired evaluation inspectable; it is not an official-engine reproduction or a release of the private policy.

Public source, protocols, executed notebooks, aggregate evidence and synthetic examples are retained. Raw replay corpora, private checkpoints, credentials, submission archives and the exact private competitive implementation remain excluded. Upstream controller and method credits remain in the [source matrix](research/leading_solution_reproduction_matrix.md).
